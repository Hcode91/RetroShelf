Drink & drive an airship, dodge fighter jets, smoke, and ram skyscrapers in a 3D arcade combat game.

Built with BlocksDS + Nitro Engine. Every sound effect and 3D model is procedurally generated from code in the repo; the ROM icon is the hand-drawn icon.png, converted to the DS banner format by tools/gen_icon.py.

Features edit

  • Survival mode: fly as long as you can while jets, birds, and a shrinking margin for error close in.
  • Destructible buildings: ram skyscrapers to wreck them for points, or fly straight through enemy jets to down them.
  • Drink and smoke: grab a beer from the cooler and hold it to your mouth to heal, at the cost of a woozy screen wobble, or light a cigarette for a temporary speed boost.
  • Local wireless multiplayer: up to 8 players in a battle royale dogfight (requires two or more real DS family consoles, does not work in the browser version).
  • Emblem editor: paint a custom design and wrap it onto both sides of your blimp's hull.
  • Hull color customization, a fully synthesized soundtrack and sound effects, and a hand drawn opening crawl.
  • Every sound effect and 3D model in this game was generated by custom tools built alongside it, all included in the source.

Controls edit

D-Pad or ABXY - Steer and float

L/R - Throttle

Drag from the center and flick - Throw a drink or cigarette

Start - Pause
