Port of Minicraft to the PSP. Originally developed by Notch for Ludum Dare in 2011, the game plays like a 2D version of Minecraft. You wake up on an island, punch trees, dig down into caves, and build a staircase to fight the Air Wizard in the sky.

The version is based on Konchunas's Minicraft PSP port, now built with the modern PSPSDK. It doesn't crash anymore. The lag during the final boss fight is gone. The screen scales right so you can actually see what's going on around you.

There are new things to do, too. You can save your game. You can craft torches to see in the dark. There are cold biomes with snow, ice, and frozen zombies if you explore far enough. And there are 60 achievements to unlock.
